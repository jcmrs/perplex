# Knowledge Base

**Purpose:** Captured learnings, research findings, and institutional memory.

## Structure

- `research/` - Research findings and investigation results
- `learnings/` - Mistakes, discoveries, patterns identified
- `external/` - Information from external sources (including Perplexity AI!)
- `patterns/` - Reusable patterns and solutions

## This vs Decisions vs Docs

- **Knowledge:** Raw findings, research, learnings
- **Decisions:** Why we chose specific paths
- **Docs:** How the system works and how to use it

## For AI Agents

- Store research findings here before they inform decisions
- Document mistakes and learnings immediately
- Reference knowledge base when making similar decisions

## For Humans

This is where AI discoveries are preserved for your review and insight.
