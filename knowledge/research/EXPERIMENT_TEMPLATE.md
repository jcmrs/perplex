**For AI Agents:**

**When planning experiment:**
- Fill out Hypothesis, Objective, Context, Design sections
- Get feedback if experiment is complex or risky
- Commit experiment plan before executing

**During experiment:**
- Take notes in Results section as you go
- Capture data immediately (don't rely on memory)
- Note surprises and unexpected behaviors

**After experiment:**
- Complete Analysis and Implications
- Update Traceability (create ADRs, requirements, etc.)
- Document Follow-up questions

**Experiments are learning tools, not production code. It's okay if experiments fail - that's valuable learning.**

---

**For Humans:**

Experiments show the AI's investigative process. Review to understand:
- What technical approaches were tested
- Why certain paths were chosen or rejected
- What constraints or opportunities were discovered
- How decisions were informed by evidence
